Why another app?

Because sharing a run got complicated.

Posting a run shouldn’t mean joining another social network. No sign-up, no username, no waitlist to get in.

And when you stop your watch, you want to post now. Not refresh, wait, and find yesterday’s run instead.

How it works

Three steps. About thirty seconds.

1 Finish your run

It arrives from Apple Health or Strava on its own. Or open a file from your watch.

2 Make it yours

Drop on distance, pace, route, splits or your week. Pick a photo, a font, a colour.

3 Post it

Straight to Instagram Stories. Or save it, or copy one sticker and paste it anywhere.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no sign-up. Your activities and designs stay on your phone.

Which watches work?

Apple Watch, plus any watch app you’ve set to save workouts to Apple Health (Garmin, Coros, Polar, Suunto, Wahoo and Amazfit apps can). Anything on Strava works too.

Can I bring in runs from other apps?

Yes. Connect Strava, or export a GPX, TCX or FIT file from any watch or app and open it in Mileprint.

Is it really free?

Yes. Everything in the app is free, with no watermark and no ads. If a paid Plus arrives, it adds extras. It won’t take anything away.

Where is my data?

On your phone. We don’t have a server that stores your runs. Read the privacy policy.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. iPhone first.
