Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Mileprint is made by YOUR NAME OR COMPANY (“we”). This policy explains what the Mileprint iPhone app does with your information. The short version: your workouts, photos and designs stay on your phone, and we don’t collect them.

What stays on your phone

  • Activities you import or sync: times, distances, routes, splits, heart rate, elevation and calories.
  • Story designs and the photos you choose as backgrounds.
  • Settings, such as units and your last-used font and colour.
  • Strava sign-in tokens, kept in the iPhone Keychain.

This information is stored in the app’s private storage on your device. We have no server that stores it, so we can’t see it.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Mileprint reads your workouts, workout routes, heart rate, distance and active energy to make story graphics. It never writes to Health. Health data is not used for advertising or marketing, is not sold, is not shared with third parties, and is not stored in iCloud by Mileprint. You can change or remove access at any time in the Health app: your profile picture → Apps → Mileprint.

Strava

If you connect Strava, the app asks Strava for read access to your activities and downloads them directly from Strava to your phone. To finish signing in, a one-time code passes through a small function we run (hosted on Supabase), which exchanges it with Strava for your access tokens and returns them to the app. That function does not store the code, the tokens or any of your data. Disconnecting Strava in Settings revokes the app’s access and deletes the activities that came from Strava from your phone.

Photos

Mileprint asks to read a photo only when you choose one as a background, and to add to your library only when you save a story. Photos are not uploaded anywhere.

Sharing

When you share to Instagram, save to Photos or use the share sheet, the image goes only where you send it. Those services’ own privacy policies then apply.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account, no analytics and no advertising. If you email us for help, we receive what you write and your email address, and use it only to reply.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes every activity, design and setting from the app and disconnects Strava. Deleting the app removes everything too.

Children

Mileprint is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above and describe the change in the app’s release notes.
