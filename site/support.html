Help

The same answers are in the app under Settings → Help and feedback.

Do I need an account?

No. Mileprint keeps your activities and designs on your phone. There is no sign-up, feed or waitlist.

My latest workout isn’t showing

Watch apps save to Apple Health a minute or two after you stop. Pull down on the activity list to sync. If it still isn’t there, open the Health app, tap your profile picture → Apps → Mileprint, and allow Workouts and Workout Routes. For Strava, check the activity has finished uploading.

Which watches and apps work?

Apple Watch, plus any watch app you’ve set to save workouts to Apple Health (Garmin, Coros, Polar, Suunto, Wahoo and Amazfit apps can). Also anything on Strava, and GPX, TCX or FIT files exported from any app.

Why do the same run’s numbers differ between apps?

Each app smooths GPS and elevation differently. Mileprint uses your device’s own totals when the file or app provides them, and works out splits from the route otherwise.

How do I post to Instagram?

In the editor, tap Instagram to open the story in Instagram, ready to post. Or open Copy stickers, tap a sticker, and paste it onto any photo in your story.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all data removes everything on your phone and disconnects Strava. Disconnecting Strava also removes the activities that came from it.
